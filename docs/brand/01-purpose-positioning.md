## Purpose

Help South Africans who juggle personal and business money see **Safe to spend** — without mistaking that number for cash in the bank, and without confusing it with plan leftover.

## One-liners (Jules fold)

**In-app / brand purpose:** Know what you can **Safe to spend** across personal and business workspaces — not cash in the bank, and not the same as plan leftover.

**Social / Limited:** Android budgeting for SA — personal and business money kept apart. Safe to spend ≠ cash in the bank.

Do **not** use “what your plan says you can still spend” as the Home STS one-liner (that maps to plan leftover, not Safe to spend).

## Beachhead (internal)

SA Android users with supported Absa and FNB **bank-alert** SMS formats who juggle personal + business or side-hustle money; small high-trust / invite-only cohort first.

## Trust pillars (product law)

1. **Safe to spend** is the number to use before you buy — not the same as bank cash alone, and not plan leftover / plan surplus.
2. SMS balances are **bank-alert signals** (supported Absa and FNB bank-alert formats — not your other messages), not a full statement reconcile.
3. Invoices never change the plan until you approve; no payment promises. (In-app honesty only — keep invoices off marketing lead until Vera Verifies.)

## Promise (Limited — public-safe)

| ID | Promise | Not this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Keep personal and business money in separate spaces on your phone | Accountant-ready books |
| P2 | Safe to spend — not the same as cash in the bank (and not plan leftover) | True cash / reconciled / what’s in the bank |
| P3 | Balances from supported Absa and FNB **bank-alert** formats (can be wrong). Reads those alerts on your phone — not linked to your bank | Connected bank / open banking / official balance / all messages |
| P4 | Invite-only early access while we prove reliability | Production-ready for everyone |

## Hold (omit from brand & social lead)

Payments; invoice inbox / forwarding as marketing lead; OCR any doc; iOS; all SA banks; audited / pen-tested; “we read your messages.”

## Brand personality

Warm, plain, honest — a careful friend, not a bank ad. Approachable professional. Lead with what it **is** and what it **isn’t** when money or trust is involved.

## Visual summary (kit v0)

Warm paper background, teal primary, Inter type. Home leads with **Safe to spend**; secondary **Plan leftover (not cash)** stays quieter. Accent gold is never used for money truth.
