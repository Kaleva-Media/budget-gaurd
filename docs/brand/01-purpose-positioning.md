# BudgetGuard — brand purpose & positioning (one-pager)

**Owner:** Priya (brand) + Jules (claims)  
**Mode:** Limited early-access / invite English  
**Status:** Draft for Jules forbidden-claims review → Gugu claims/UX  
**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Product name:** BudgetGuard (locked)

---

## Purpose

Help South Africans who juggle personal and business money see **what their plan says they can still spend** — without mistaking that number for cash in the bank.

## Positioning one-liner (Jules)

Android budgeting for South Africans who keep personal and business money in separate spaces — what your plan says you can still spend, not cash in the bank.

## Beachhead (internal)

SA Android users with Absa / supported FNB bank-alert SMS who juggle personal + business or side-hustle money; small high-trust / invite-only cohort first.

## Promise (Limited — public-safe)

| ID | Promise | Not this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Keep personal and business money in separate spaces on your phone | Accountant-ready books |
| P2 | What your plan says you can still spend — not the same as cash in the bank | True cash / reconciled / what’s in the bank |
| P3 | Balances from Absa / limited FNB SMS alerts (can be wrong). Reads bank SMS on your phone — not linked to your bank | Connected bank / open banking / official balance |
| P4 | Invite-only early access while we prove reliability | Production-ready for everyone |

## Hold (omit from brand & social)

Payments; invoice inbox / forwarding; OCR any doc; iOS; all SA banks; audited / pen-tested; “we read your messages.”

## Brand personality

Warm, plain, honest — a careful friend, not a bank ad. Approachable professional. Lead with what it **is** and what it **isn’t** when money or trust is involved.

## Visual summary (kit v0)

Warm paper background, teal primary, Inter type. Home leads with **Safe to spend**; secondary **Plan leftover (not cash)** stays quieter. Accent gold is never used for money truth.
